Corridor throughput under three fare regimes

Twelve thousand agents choosing between four modes on one corridor, run under a flat fare, a distance fare and a peak-priced fare. I wanted to know which regime moves the most people, not which earns the most.

Mechanism, in code

for regime in ('flat', 'distance', 'peak'):
    for sim in range(300):
        agents = spawn(12_000, income=lognorm())
        for t in range(0, 90):
            for a in agents:
                a.choose(cost(regime, a), wait(t), crowd(t))
            board(vehicles=240, dwell=dwell(crowd(t)))
        record(regime, throughput())
04

What the run changed

What I had not modelled

Peak pricing wins on throughput by 10% over a flat fare, but for a reason I had not modelled: it thins the shoulder, which cuts dwell time, which is the real cap. The fare is acting on boarding speed, not on demand.
