About

I spent years building systems. Now I study them.

futurist. systems engineering. building infra one step at a time.

Lead engineer across seven companies in four countries: a property marketplace, agricultural traceability on Stellar, a court and trial management system, a mobile bank, four products at an accelerator. Building inside those systems is where the questions came from, and none of the tools I had were the ones that answer them.

So I am changing what I do. A six-month intensive, ninety to a hundred and twenty minutes a day, six days a week. I still ship software. It is no longer the point.

I am not picking one field yet. More evidence first, then one system gets the next decade.
